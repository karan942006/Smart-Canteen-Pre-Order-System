Smart Canteen Pre-Order System
Project Title
Smart Canteen Pre-Order System
1. Introduction
The Smart Canteen Pre-Order System is a web-based application designed to simplify food ordering in college canteens. Students can place food orders online before reaching the canteen, reducing waiting time and improving service efficiency. The system helps both students and canteen staff by digitizing the ordering process.
2. Problem Statement
In traditional canteen systems:
Students spend a lot of time waiting in queues.
Order management becomes difficult during peak hours.
Wrong or missed orders may occur.
Canteen staff face challenges handling large numbers of customers.
Lunch breaks are often wasted due to delays.
3. Proposed Solution
The Smart Canteen Pre-Order System allows students to browse the menu, select food items, and place orders online. The canteen staff receive orders in advance and prepare them before the student arrives. The system also provides order tracking and token generation for smooth order collection.
4. Objectives
Reduce waiting time in canteen queues.
Improve order management and accuracy.
Digitize the food ordering process.
Enhance student convenience and satisfaction.
Increase operational efficiency of the canteen.

6. System Workflow
Student logs into the system.
Student views the menu.
Student selects food items.
Student places an order.
System generates a unique token number.
Canteen staff receive the order.
Food is prepared.
Order status is updated.
Student collects the order using the token number.
Order is marked as completed.
7. Order Status Flow
Pending → Accepted → Preparing → Ready → Delivered
Example:
Order ID: 101
Status: Pending
Status: Accepted
Status: Preparing
Status: Ready
Status: Delivered
